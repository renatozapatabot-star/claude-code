The founder's thesis: **Adjunto and CRUZ are free lead-gen** — beautifully designed proof surfaces that
make the FREIGHT services (Renato Zapata & Co, Norfleet Logistics, and the rest) easier to sell. The
websites, emails, and the founder's LinkedIn network are the growth assets. **The trust laws bind
hardest here:** every outbound is **drafted, never auto-sent**; no spam; every send stays
founder-signed (canon §0.4-8). `canon-check` enforces that every stage below names an
artifact-producing skill and a channel.

## §Proof — the shareable artifact (P7-W2-01)

Standing rule: every completed shipment can emit a client-facing *"here's what FREIGHT saved / did"*
artifact — `cost-optimizer` savings + `financial-summary`, branded to the white+red canon, rendered as
an Artifact or PDF, ready for the founder to share. Template checklist:

1. Client + tráfico header, white+red canon, `DATOS DEMO` watermark until figures are real. Charts
   and savings meters rendered through the `dataviz` system (one visual language with the Deck).
2. Savings figure (`saved_mxn` / `saved_usd`) from the pipeline §Savings hook.
3. Compliance status (MVE E2 current) + cleared documents.
4. One-line CTA to the FREIGHT service, founder-signed.

## §Outreach — the LinkedIn/email kit (P7-W2-02, EXEC-NOW draft-only)

`client-communication-writer` + Gmail drafts + `deep-research` produce personalized, founder-reviewed
outreach drafts from the LinkedIn network. **Nothing sends without the founder's click.** [WO-09]
(licensed-authority packet) gates only *consequential automated* sends — which §0.4-8 already keeps
human — so the **drafts flow now**; the packet is not a blocker to staging them.
