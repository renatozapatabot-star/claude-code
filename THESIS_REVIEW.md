## Changes applied in this revision

1. **New chapter — "When the Method Fails Anyway"** (closes Part II). Luck, survivorship bias, structural barriers; the 90-day diagnosis (route / ground / draw); appeals as statistics; keeps the floor rituals during a cold streak. Closes the largest gap: the draft quietly implied failure = mis-execution.
2. **New section — "The Debt You Already Carry"** (Part II, before the traps). Triage order, judgments vs. collectors, statute-of-limitations clock-restart warning, garnishment limits, debt validation, nonprofit counseling vs. paid "repair," bankruptcy as a legal reset.
3. **Structural bypass:** one line after the 14 lines routes the crisis reader straight to Part II past the manifesto.
4. **Safety patches:** lost-tolerance overdose warning ("the old dose is a new dose") in both the routing table and the doors list; new **under-18 routing row** (youth shelters, runaway helplines, trafficking line, "no real door charges that"); US worked example gains the National Runaway Safeline + Safe Place.
5. **Utility patches:** email/password recovery added to the pocket-card floor; "Clean is a tool" hygiene paragraph (showers, laundry, clothing closets) in the 72 hours; benefits-cliff warning in Days 31–60; the minimum-viable day promoted to "The low-power day" — an explicit depression-mode track, not a fallback.
6. **Factual trims:** passport claim brought to true strength (opens most borders; not "among the most powerful ever printed"; names the door due north it doesn't open); 112 locked-phone claim carved for SIM-less-blocking countries.
7. **Craft trim:** the Close cut ~30% — recap paragraphs condensed to two, scales paragraph tightened — so the final "Go" lands from silence.
8. **Audience patch:** Part III's "read your own map" now pays a down payment on the translation — parallel touchpoints for a woman reader running the same method on her own map.
9. **New chapter — "Player One"** (Part III, before "What No Final Name Can Hold"). The simulation subthesis: the question is sealed from the inside and self-concealing (player one is never told, and cannot even tell itself); the method is invariant under it (every page survives both readings); the one-run maximization argument (access is exactly one episode wide, so play greedy on the authored objective — unscored value is never scored, by anyone, ever); and the asymmetric wager — play it for keeps, refusing "it's not real" as anesthetic. Echoed in one line in the Close.
10. **New companion — `THIS_BRIEF_FLAME_FIELD_EDITION.md`:** plain-language (~6th-grade) printable edition of the field manual: floor checklist, gates, four routes, two questions, anti-scam rules, debt triage, daily loop, doors list, US quick list. This is the copy a caseworker hands someone — and the move that makes the project, as opposed to the file, a 100.
