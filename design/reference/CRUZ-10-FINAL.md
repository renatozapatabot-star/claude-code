# CRUZ — The True 10/10 Plan (final synthesis)
*Merges the "two-rooms" design specification with the road-to-10 gaps and the guer/SMB positioning. This is the definitive target. It is a deliberate aesthetic redirection of what we've built — toward something more ownable, heritage-rooted, and impossible for the incumbents to copy.*

---

## 0. POSITIONING — why we win (the lens every pixel serves)
We are **not** VC-funded enterprise software pretending to be big. Our edge is **guerrilla**: a real **brokerage license** (Patente 3596 · Aduana 240 · EST. 1941, US+MX, warehouse/logistics) + **design + workflow so good it spreads itself.** Think **Rose Rocket / Mercury**: the up-and-comer that looks like the *future of the category* and **10×'s an SMB brokerage's capacity** — so good that enterprises want it, though SMBs are the target.
- **Design is the acquisition channel.** Every artifact a supplier/client sees (portal, status email, the cleared-document PDF) must be screenshot-and-forward good. The viral loop *is* the GTM.
- **Looking nothing like GlobalPC/SLAM (25–37-yr-old software) is positioning, not vanity.** A calm, beautiful, modern surface is a moat they structurally won't cross.
- **The license is the moat the design must dramatize** — hence the brass Patente seal as the signature element.

## 1. THE REDIRECTION (the big call)
Adopt **"the building — two rooms, shared materials, lit differently."** This **replaces the navy/cyan generic-AI-dashboard direction** (which the 2026 research warns reads as sameness) with the brokerage's **own materials**: **brass** (the patente seal/trust), **ledger hairlines** (the accounting register), and the **real semáforo** (verde/ámbar/rojo) as the status system.
- **The Archive (client):** warm reading-room — paper `#FAF7F0`, ink `#1C1A17`, brass `#B08D57`. Editorial, calm, permanent. Clients never see customs machinery.
- **The Console (broker/operator):** focused operations room — graphite `#1A1917`, surface `#242220`, ink `#ECE7DD`, brass `#C9A063`. Dense-but-quiet, Linear-restrained — **not a cockpit**.
- **Shared:** brass seal · 0.5px ledger rules (primary structural device, not boxes) · semáforo as the only status color.
> **Honest caveat — typography:** the spec names **Fraunces** for the heritage serif; our craft guidance flags Fraunces as over-used. Recommendation: keep the *intent* (a characterful, old-style display serif used sparingly) but evaluate **Fraunces vs. an alternative** (e.g. a more distinctive editorial serif) in a 1-screen bake-off before committing. **Inter** for UI and a **mono** for every identifier stand.

## 2. TOKENS (the re-skin)
Re-token the spine from navy/cyan → **graphite/brass/semáforo**:
- Console: `--app-bg:#1A1917 · --surface:#242220 · --text:#ECE7DD · --text-muted:#8A8478 · --rule:#33302C · --brass:#C9A063 · verde:#5FA46B · ambar:#D4A14A · rojo:#C75D4A`.
- Archive: `--paper:#FAF7F0 · --ink:#1C1A17 · --ink-muted:#6B655C · --brass:#B08D57 · --rule:#E5DFD3 · sage/amber/clay`.
- **Color = semáforo + confidence only. Brass = trust/identity only. No decorative color.**
- Type scale 12/13/15/18/24/32, **two weights (400/500)**, sentence case, ALL-CAPS only for 12px letter-spaced eyebrows. Radius 8 (12 cards); **the seal is the only circle**.
*Acceptance: grep shows zero `#22D3EE`/navy in component markup; one brass token; semáforo drives all status.*

## 3. THE SIGNATURE — the brass Patente seal
Build **`PatenteSeal`**: an embossed brass medallion — "PATENTE 3596 · ADUANA 240 · EST. 1941." Appears on **login**, on **every cleared-document PDF**, and quietly in the Archive corner. This is the "real, signed, legitimate" anchor and the screenshot-bait. **Spend the boldness here; keep everything else disciplined.**

## 4. THE GATE — the crown jewel (corresponsabilidad surface)
Tito's signature carries legal weight, so the Gate must give **genuine basis in ~10 seconds** and **fight automation complacency** (the dominant failure mode: a reliable-looking system gets rubber-stamped until oversight is theater). One entrada at a time:
1. **What it is** — client + tráfico (mono).
2. **What CRUZ did** (tool-use disclosure) — tight checklist: HTS classified (code+confidence), docs n/n validated, MVE verified (E2), USMCA applied (savings), each green-checked.
3. **Confidence** — one bar + number (context for the flag).
4. **The flag — the *why*** — a single amber callout naming exactly what needs his criterion, tied to legal stakes: *"Valor declarado 18% sobre el histórico del proveedor. Bajo corresponsabilidad, requiere tu criterio antes de transmitir."*
5. **One primary action** — ✅ Aprobar y transmitir · quiet Rechazar / Pedir aclaración. Pause/veto is first-class.
*Acceptance: a reviewer can defend the decision in ~10s; routine never reaches here; the flag always states a why, never "review needed."*

## 5. THE FIVE AGENTIC PATTERNS (mapped — this is the "frontier" substance)
1. **Planning visibility** → the **pipeline strip** (Ingesta → Clasificación → Validación → Costeo → Borrador → Compuerta → VUCEM → Semáforo → Despacho) with live counts.
2. **Tool-use disclosure** → "what CRUZ did" checklist on every entrada + at the Gate.
3. **Confidence visualization** → score on every classification/draft; low auto-routes to the Gate; the flag shows *why*.
4. **Multi-step workflow tracking** → the queue/board, all entradas by stage, live, with *"CRUZ procesó 10 sin intervención."*
5. **Recovery routing** → every failure (rejected pedimento, rojo, missing doc) routes to a clear next action; errors **direct, never apologize** ("Falta la factura comercial — súbela aquí para continuar.").
- **Cross-cutting: progressive delegation** — as accuracy proves out, more categories auto-clear; the approval history sets the pace of autonomy. The system **earns** permission.

## 6. THE TWO SURFACES (build targets)
- **Archive (client):** hero = **one Fraunces status line** ("Todo despejado." / "2 embarques requieren tu atención."), no KPIs/charts/jargon; below = the **records hall** (6 expediente tabs as a browsable permanent library, each a card with a brass "cleared" mark); shipment view = plain-language timeline + semáforo + downloadable sealed docs. Spanish-first, EN toggle.
- **Console (broker/operator):** left rail (Entradas · Cola · Pedimentos · Bodega · Tráficos · Clientes); lead with **4 numbers** (Entradas hoy · Despejadas · En pipeline · **Requiere aprobación** in amber); then pipeline strip; then the auto-processing queue. Calm, Linear-restrained.

## 7. FOLD-IN: the road-to-10 gaps (kept, re-expressed in the new language)
- **Pills → semáforo chips** (one recipe; color always paired with label/icon — colorblind-safe).
- **Type/8-pt discipline** → the 12–32 scale, two weights, ledger spacing.
- **Real reasoning (B1)** → the "what CRUZ did" checklist is *generated per load* via `window.claude.complete`; varies by entrada.
- **Autonomy governs (B3)** → progressive delegation wired to the dial + approval history.
- **Append-only audit (B4)** → every approve/reject/cancel logged, queryable ("why did SHP-44310 clear?").
- **Two-sided live moment (B6)** → one GO ripples Console + Archive at once.
- **Mobile-first** (the corridor runs on phones), keyboard focus, reduced-motion, mono+selectable identifiers — the non-negotiable quality floor.
