# THIS BRIEF FLAME — FIELD EDITION

**The short, plain version. Print it. Fold it. Hand it to someone.**
*The full book has the why. This has the what. Simple words on purpose — read it tired, read it scared, read it in your second language.*

---

## READ THIS FIRST

If you might hurt yourself, or you are not safe tonight, that comes first.

- Call your country's **emergency number** (US: 911. Most of Europe: 112. UK: 999. Australia: 000).
- In the US, call or text **988** — the crisis line. Free. Any hour. You can text if you can't talk.
- Other countries: search "**[your country] crisis line**." Save the number tonight.

You don't have to fix your life tonight. Your only job tonight is to stay.

If reading is hard, hand this page to a librarian, a nurse, or anyone at a food line and say: **"Read me the next step."** That is allowed. That is how this works.

---

## STEP 1 — TONIGHT (protect the floor)

Do these in order. Check them off.

- [ ] **A safe place to sleep tonight.** In the US, dial **211** and ask. Anywhere else: search "[your city] emergency shelter" or ask at a church, temple, or mosque.
- [ ] **Water, food, today's medicine.** Food banks and community kitchens are free. No application. No shame.
- [ ] **Kids, elders, and animals in your care are safe.**
- [ ] **Charge your phone.** It is your lifeline.
- [ ] **Keep your ID.** Never give your original ID, phone, or passwords to anyone. Take photos of your documents.
- [ ] **Keep your email working.** If you lose your phone number, email is how you get your accounts back.
- [ ] **Find the one deadline that can take your home, your license, your kids, or your freedom.** A court paper beats every phone call. Handle the paper first.

**Warning:** If you take insulin, heart pills, seizure pills, or mental-health pills — or you drink heavily every day — do NOT stop suddenly. Stopping some of these can kill. Tell a pharmacist or clinic TODAY. They can help for free or cheap.

**If someone at home hurts you:** call the domestic violence line from a phone they can't see (US: 1-800-799-7233). Let them plan your exit with you. Leaving is the most dangerous moment — plan it, don't rush it.

## STEP 2 — NAME WHAT'S IN YOUR WAY

Say it plainly. No shame. It changes the route, not your worth.

Papers or work permit · a criminal record · your body or health · depression or anxiety · drink or drugs · kids or elders you care for · no car or bus money · language · no ID · you're under 18.

- **Under 18?** Adult shelters may turn you away. Search "[your city] youth shelter." US: call or text **1-800-RUNAWAY**. Look for yellow **Safe Place** signs on buses and stores.
- **Record?** Some employers hire anyway. In the US, say in the interview: "There are federal programs that pay employers to hire me." (Federal Bonding Program, WOTC.)
- **Disabled?** Apply for disability benefits on DAY ONE, not later. The date you file protects your back pay.
- **No papers?** Only get advice from a real lawyer or accredited nonprofit. Never buy documents. Never pay a "notario." Wage theft can still be reported — you can often get stolen pay whatever your status.
- **Drink or drugs?** Free same-day help exists: search "[your country] addiction helpline." Ask a pharmacy for **naloxone (Narcan)** — it reverses an opioid overdose. Never use alone. **After jail, detox, or time clean, your old dose can kill you.** Use less, slower, never alone.

---

## STEP 3 — FIRST MONEY THIS WEEK (pick ONE)

1. **CLAIM what is already yours.** Unpaid wages. Unemployment. Food benefits (US: apply for SNAP and say *"I want to request expedited service"* — it can arrive in 7 days). Tax refunds. Deposits.
2. **SELL something you don't need.** Honest photos. Price it like the other sold ones. Meet in a public place. Cash only. Never take "overpayment."
3. **SERVE — do one small job.** Cleaning, hauling, yard work, moving help. Say this: *"I haul for people moving. First load $40. I can come Saturday."* Agree on price BEFORE you start. Get it in a text.
4. **SHIFT — call a staffing agency.** Call, don't just apply online. Ask: "What shift starts soonest? What's the rate? What exact day is first pay?"

**Before any job or deal, ask two questions:**
1. *If this goes as wrong as it can, do I get back up — and does anyone else get hurt?* If no way back — **walk away. No prize is worth ruin.**
2. *Can I undo it?* Yes → do it today. No → wait and ask someone you trust.

---

## STEP 4 — DON'T GET ROBBED

If it costs you money before it pays you money — **it is robbing you.**

- Never pay to get a job.
- Never deposit a check and send part of the money back. The check is fake. You will owe it all.
- Never buy gift cards or crypto for a "boss."
- Never reship packages or let someone "run money through your account." That is a crime — YOURS.
- Never sign a title loan or payday rollover. Never cosign when you're broke.
- Get scope, price, and pay day in writing (a text message counts) before you work.
- Track your hours. Stolen wages can be recovered — save the proof.

## STEP 5 — THE DEBT YOU ALREADY HAVE

Pay in this order, not in the order of who yells loudest:

1. **Debts that take your floor:** rent, the car you work from, utilities, child support.
2. **Taxes:** ask for a payment plan before they come to you.
3. **Old cards, medical bills, collections:** loudest voices, weakest teeth.

- A court paper is the only letter that can really hurt you fast. **Answer it. Show up. Always.**
- Old debt can expire. A small payment can restart the clock. **Get free advice before paying anything on old debt.** (US: nonprofit help at nfcc.org. Free legal aid: LawHelp.org.)
- Bankruptcy is a legal tool, not a shame. Ask legal aid if and when.
- Anyone charging money up front to "fix your credit" is a scam.

## EVERY DAY (even the worst ones)

1. **Floor check (10 min):** safe, fed, meds, kids, phone charged, next deadline.
2. **Money block (1 hour):** calls, offers, work.
3. **Follow up (20 min):** push every "maybe" to yes, no, or a date.
4. **Learn (30 min):** practice the one skill you're building.
5. **Close (10 min):** write down money in, money out, one good thing, tomorrow's first call — with a time. "Call the agency at 9."

**On a bad day, do the small version:** stay safe, do one money thing, talk to one safe person, sleep. That is a full day. Ten of those in a row is a bridge. A missed day is not a debt. You just start again tomorrow. **There is no behind.**

**No money after 14 days?** The route is wrong, not you. Pick a different route from Step 3. Ask a caseworker or librarian to look at your plan. Appeal every "no" from a benefits office — many first answers are wrong, and appeals win.

## WHERE THE HELP IS (any country)

Ask any of these people: *"I have no money and I need help — where do I start?"* They answer this every week:

**A librarian · a hospital social worker · a pharmacist · a nurse · a teacher · a worker center · a church, temple, or mosque.**

Doors that exist almost everywhere — search these words:
- "[your city] food bank" · "[your city] shelter"
- "[your country] crisis line" · "[your country] social services"
- "[your city] free legal aid" · "[your city] free clinic"
- "[your country] replace ID" · "[your city] employment center"
- "[your city] clothing closet" (free interview clothes) — and shelters, rec centers, and truck stops have showers. Clean clothes are work equipment. It's OK to ask.

**US quick list:** 211 (everything) · 988 (crisis) · SNAP + "expedited service" (food) · Medicaid (health, any day) · HealthCare.gov (after job loss) · state unemployment office · American Job Centers (free job help) · LawHelp.org (free lawyers) · VITA (free tax filing — the EITC can be worth thousands) · school enrolls a homeless child same day, no papers needed.

## THE DEAL, IN FOUR LINES

1. **Your account is at zero. You are not zero.**
2. Protect the floor: safety, food, meds, phone, ID, the one deadline.
3. Never trade the way back — your ID, your body, your honesty, your people — for fast money.
4. Then aim higher than surviving. The floor was never the point. Your life is.

**Get up. Make one call. Today counts.**
